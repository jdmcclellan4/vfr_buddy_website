Privacy policy

Last updated 22 Aug 2026
Operator Flight Forge LLC

This policy applies to the VFR Buddy Android app published on Google Play by Flight Forge LLC (and the matching iOS app if listed later). It describes how we access, use, store, and share information. Contact: vfrbuddy@outlook.com.

What we access and store

On your device Settings only

Favorites, personal minimums, refresh intervals, recent searches (Pro), and onboarding flags are stored on your device. We do not operate a VFR Buddy server that receives this information.

Location Optional

Precise location is used only if you choose “nearest weather.” The app sends a geographic bounding box to NOAA’s public Aviation Weather Center API to find nearby METARs. We do not store a location history, and you can search by ICAO or city without granting location.

Network NOAA AWC

Weather requests (METAR, TAF, SIGMET, and related public aviation data) go over HTTPS to the NOAA/NWS Aviation Weather Center. Requests include airport identifiers you search or save. NOAA may log technical data such as IP address on their systems; we do not control NOAA’s logs.

Sharing, selling, and ads

We do not sell your information. We do not use advertising SDKs or analytics SDKs. The only third party we send data to for app functionality is NOAA/NWS AWC, as described above. Google Play (and your device OS) may process crash or install diagnostics under their own policies.

Analytics None
Advertising None
Account Not required

Children

VFR Buddy is rated Everyone and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Retention and deletion

On-device settings remain until you change them or uninstall the app. Uninstalling removes local data. We do not keep a cloud copy of your favorites or minimums. See Delete data for more.

Purchases and sign-in

The current Google Play listing does not require a VFR Buddy account. VFR Buddy Pro is a $1.99 per year auto-renewing subscription billed by Google Play. We do not receive your payment card number. Google may share subscription status with the app so Pro features can unlock. Sign in with Google is not required for this build. This policy will be updated if we add accounts.

Privacy questions: vfrbuddy@outlook.com
