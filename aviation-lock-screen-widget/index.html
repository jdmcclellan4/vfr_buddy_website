VFR Buddy Pro puts METAR data from your favorite airport (up to 5) on the home or lock screen displaying current conditions at a glance.

METAR widget for Android and iOS (VFR Buddy Pro)

The widget is built for pilots who want an aviation weather lock screen with airport identifier, color-coded flight category, and a short METAR-style summary (visibility, clouds, wind). It is the same color system as the app - green: VFR, blue: MVFR, red: IFR, violet: LIFR, orange: below your personal minimums.

What the VFR Buddy Pro lock screen widget shows

  • Up to 5 favorite airports on one widget
  • Color-coded flight categories
  • Ceiling, visibility, and wind in a compact format
  • Home screen and lock screen layouts (Pro)

The aviation lock screen widget is part of VFR Buddy Pro at $4.99/year.
