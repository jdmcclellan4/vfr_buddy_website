How to set VFR personal minimums

Personal minimums are YOUR go / no-go numbers, not just the flight category. VFR Buddy turns those numbers into an orange alert on the favorites list so you know whether to continue preflight planning... or fire up the simulator instead.

What personal minimums are (and are not)

For VFR pilots (sport pilots, light sport aircraft, paramotor, ultralight etc), VFR weather minimums are the legal floor. Personal minimums are what you deem appropriate for the pilot, mission and aircraft - often different from VFR. There is no universal standard for personal minimums, but having your own is part of good Aeronautical Decision Making.

Customizable Personal Minimums in VFR Buddy

  • Ceiling (ft) - minimum ceiling height in feet AGL
  • Visibility (SM) - minimum distance at which prominent objects can be identified
  • Max wind (kt) - maximum sustained wind
  • Max gust (kt) - maximum gust; critical for PPG and ultralight

Defaults in the app are VFR-style starting points (for example 3,000 ft ceiling and 5 SM). Change them to suit the pilot and aircraft. A paramotor pilot’s gust number will not match a high-performance GA airplane.

A simple way to pick starting values

Start with what you already use on a typical local flight or as recommended by a CFI. If the METAR is below your set personal minimums, VFR Buddy color-codes that airport orange instead of the usual VFR/MVFR/IFR/LIFR color.

Confirm with a real briefing

If your airport is coded in orange, go bowling. If it's coded in green, continue preflight planning with an official brief.
