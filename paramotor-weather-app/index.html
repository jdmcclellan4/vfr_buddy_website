Paramotor weather app for PPG and ultralight pilots

Foot-launched and light aircraft care about wind and gusts first. VFR Buddy is a VFR weather app with adjustable personal minimums allowing you to customize beyond standard VFR minimums.

Why paramotor and ultralight weather is different

A 15-knot gust that is routine for a Cessna can be a no-go for a paramotor. PPG and ultralight pilots often need a weather view that highlights wind and gust values,, not just flight category. VFR Buddy lets you customize your personal minimum ceiling, visibility, max wind, and max gust values. If a METAR is below your personal minimums, it codes orange so you see it before you load the wing.

PPG wind app features in VFR Buddy

  • Favorites list with ceiling, visibility, and wind on one row
  • Custom gust limits, perfect for paramotor and ultralight operations
  • METAR and TAF without ads
  • Home and lock screen METAR widget (VFR Buddy Pro)

How to use it before a PPG flight

Save the airports you actually use to your favorites (3 in FREE version, UNLIMITED in PRO). Set wind and gust values to your personal limits. On the Home Screen or Widget: green / blue / red / violet are FAA-style flight categories; orange means below your minimums. Tap an airport for full METAR, TAF, NOTAM, and SIGMET information. If it looks good, proceed with an official briefing, otherwise: fire up the flight sim.
